BEHIND THE DATA

Detection is not confirmation.

TerraSignal maps thermal anomalies supplied by NASA FIRMS. They can indicate fire activity, but may also reflect agricultural burning, other hot sources, or non-wildfire events. A satellite's absence of detections does not establish that an area is safe.

Why precision and recall matter

Missing a true fire and generating an unnecessary alert have different costs. Evaluating those tradeoffs requires independently verified fire and non-fire examples, including fires missed by the upstream satellite detector. This map alone cannot calculate false-negative rates.
